Motivation

Terminal commands are only part of autonomous work. Our long-horizon trajectories connect exploration, evidence-based judgment, strategy revision, and verification into a complete problem-solving process.

Method

Two stages turn professional problems into executable tasks that elicit—and entangle—long-horizon behavioral patterns.
